About us

Old-school Irani cafe, right here in Dighi.

Red check tablecloths, bentwood chairs, an old telephone booth in the corner and a pot of chai that never really goes off the stove. Good Luck Cafe is our take on the Irani cafes Pune grew up with.

Come early for chai and bun maska, stay for kheema pav and biryani. Nothing fancy, just honest food at fair prices.

Cafe Rules

  1. Chai first. Everything else later.
  2. Dip the bun. It's allowed here.
  3. No arguing over the bill. Split it.
  4. Come back tomorrow.
